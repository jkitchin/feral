## Findings on the Krylov proposal (in progress)

- The proposal's SPD preconditioner `M = P Ds⁻¹ L |D| Lᵀ Ds⁻¹ Pᵀ` makes
  `M⁻¹K` sign-similar to the identity: eigenvalues exactly ±1,
  `||(M⁻¹K)² − I||_F = 1.5e-10`, and preconditioned MINRES converges in **2
  iterations**. With a current-matrix factorization no Krylov subspace is
  built, so there is nothing to extract Ritz vectors from — Phase 1 and Phase
  2 of the proposal undercut each other.
- A factorization frozen one barrier decade earlier is already useless as a
  preconditioner in a synthetic model (spectrum spreads from {±1} to
  `[1.4e-2, 2.5e6]`, no convergence in 200 iterations). The model is
  deliberately harsh; this bounds the sensitivity rather than settling it.
- Relevant baseline from the 0.17.0 corpus run: `solve/MUMPS` geomean **0.08**
  against `factor/MUMPS` geomean **0.45**. Solve is already a small fraction of
  the work, which caps what any solve-side-only feature can win.
