## Addendum — issue #58: batched iterative refinement

After the checkpoint above, fixed issue #58 (a direct follow-up to fix
#2). `Solver::solve_many_refined` looped the single-RHS refiner per
column, bypassing the panel kernel — so with refinement on (default)
batched refined solves were 3–7× slower per RHS than unrefined batched.

- **`2369fce`** — new `solve_sparse_many_refined`: batched initial +
  per-step correction via `solve_sparse_many` over the active columns,
  per-column `symv` residual, per-column best-iterate + done-tracking
  (identical predicates to the single-RHS refiner), active-column
  compaction each step. Threshold dispatch `BLAS3_REFINE_THRESHOLD = 16`
  (narrow stays per-column, bit-identical). Bit-identical parity proven
  (`max|batched − per-column| == 0` at nrhs=24 SPD and nrhs=20 indef);
  nrhs=64 matches the oracle <1e-10 + residual <1e-10. Lib 317 pass,
  multi_rhs 13 pass, clippy/fmt clean. `bench_multirhs` refined section:
  batched/per-column-loop ratio ~0.34–0.40 (~2.5–3× faster).
- **`92b8150`** — `bench_dense_multirhs` (issue #58 dense-Hessian repro).

Decisions in `dev/decisions.md` 2026-05-30 (second entry); research in
`dev/research/issue-58-batched-refinement.md`.
