**`cargo run --bin bench --release` still has not been run against the new
corpus, and no factor-ratio-vs-MUMPS number is quoted anywhere in this

bit-identical on 7 of 9 corpus families, so a ratio-vs-MUMPS comparison would

What *was* measured: steady-state numeric factor time, paired, on 135 real KKT
matrices across 9 families, gated on cross-arm inertia agreement and a refined
residual. Those numbers are throughout.

## Accomplished

### `ScotchND` and `KahipND` had the identical defect, now fixed

All three ND backends refined a 2-way edge bisection through uncoarsening and
built the node separator once at the finest level. Elimination flops on the
collocation KKT at n=224,646:

| backend | before | after | gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| `MetisND` | 2.095e10 | 6.737e9 | 3.11x *(2026-09-17)* |
| `ScotchND` | 2.264e10 | 1.017e10 | 2.23x |
| `KahipND` | 2.577e10 | 7.184e9 | 3.59x |

KaHIP's symbolic analysis roughly halves (5,263 -> 2,274 ms) because its
max-flow lift now runs on the coarsest graph. Corpus wall-clock is neutral for
both (scotch 1.060 -> 1.045, kahip 1.118 -> 1.102 against `Amf`).

### A real corpus, without AMPL

`scripts/build-pounce-corpus.sh`. pounce's `generate_nl.py` builds six large
NLPs in Pyomo and writes `.nl` directly, so the AMPL Community Edition licence
is not needed. 135 matrices, 9 families, covering the shapes the argument
turns on: `bratu` (#67's `bratu3d`), `poisson` (#73's `cont5_1_l`),
`optcontrol` (#73's `dtoc2`), `laptime` (collocation, the #203 class),
`sparseqp`, `poisson450`, `rosenbrock`, and the cached `clnlbeam` / `corkscrw`.

### `Solver::with_ordering_race` — measure instead of predict

Opt-in, off by default. Ranks arms on **steady state** (second factorization,
symbolic cached), not the first call. Declines when arms disagree on inertia.
Break-even ~15 factorizations of a pattern; measured ~4.6x per factorization
on the issue #203 KKT.

### The `laptime` anomaly resolved — not a defect

Condition estimate 3.404e20 on the disputed iterates against 1.826e7 on
`laptime_0000`, where every arm *and* MA57 agree exactly. The rest was a
pivot-threshold artefact (agrees at `pivot_threshold >= 1e-6`) and my harness
gating on the unrefined `Solver::solve`.

## Things I got wrong, and corrected

Four, all mine, all found by measuring something I had asserted:

1. **"never worse, and is not slower"** shipped into `CHANGELOG.md` and the
   `node_refine` doc comment on the strength of fill alone. Timing showed
   grid2d 5.8% *slower* with 10.3% less fill. Corrected in both places and on
   the issue.
2. **"`Amd` beats `Auto` on all six families, geomean 1.17x"** was a sampling
   artefact — one iterate per family, carried by a single `laptime` matrix at
   2.02x. Over 45 matrices it is 1.067 at p=0.37, not significant.
3. **"#67/#73 vindicated, `Amf` beats `MetisND` at p=0.0079"** was measured on
   the unrefined solve path. pounce uses `solve_refined_into`; on the refined
   path it is 0.973 at p=0.2559, not significant. Neither dominates.
4. **`OrderingMethod::AutoRace` already existed** and I reported it as absent,
   having read only part of the enum, then built a second race feature. It
   measures *best of anything tried* (1.116 geomean, worst 0.931, p=0.0139).

The through-line: fill mispredicted the wall-clock winner five separate times
this session, including twice in favour of my own change. The repo already had
this on file in `tried-and-rejected.md`. Symbolic evidence is good for finding
an effect and not for claiming one.
