## Unfavourable results
- The parallel bound is loose: 1.72× (AMF) and 2.97× (AMD) of the
  measured peak. Per-worker frontal pools (up to 2× each task's largest
  front) dominate on AMD. That is why the budget switches drivers instead
  of refusing.
- AMD sequential margin is thin (1.003×).
- Delayed pivots are not modelled. The KKT test case has 15 delays and is
  still bounded.
- Not run on the KKT corpus (unavailable here). The packed parallel
  blocks and the recycle rule are timing-neutral-or-better on Primal2
  only. Maintainer A/B on the corpus (e.g. powerflow22, PDE2, marine_1600)
  is advisable.

## Decisions Made
- `peak_bytes` = predicted sequential high-water; parallel driver holds
  packed blocks; close-fit contribution recycling; over-parallel-bound
  budget → sequential driver (decisions.md 2026-11-24, #249).

## Abandoned Approaches
- Refusing on the parallel bound (committed in 5895c4b, replaced in 1e51c36).
- A 2× recycled-capacity allowance (bound went to 45.5 GB on AMD).
- A lengths-only contribution walk (under-predicted AMD by 0.5%).
(tried-and-rejected.md 2026-11-24.)
