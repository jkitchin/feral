### Item B — FT sparse LU update allocations (`c83bd0b`)

Pooled the nine sites the issue names plus two it misses: the dense
`SparseLu::update` wrapper's entering-column `collect()`, and the row buffer
`rollback` dropped instead of returning to `row_pool`. `restore_update_pools`
mirrors the existing `restore_elim_pools`; the sub-diagonal heap is cleared on
*restore*, not entry, because a mid-sweep `NeedsRefactor` leaves ranks queued.

| | allocs/update | reallocs | bytes |
|---|---|---|---|
| before | 11.2 | 8.3 | 5978 |
| after | **2.8** | 6.3 | **1521** |

The probe's own header claimed "~82 allocs/update" — stale by two pooling
rounds; corrected.

Rather than guess at the residual 2.8, I wrote a throwaway
backtrace-attributing allocator. Three sites, nothing else: `ops.push`
(retained in `self.etas`, unpoolable — the issue calls it legitimate), and
one-time growth of the `saved_pool` free list and the `u_above[r]` index as
FT fill accumulates. The churn floor is reached.

**Bit-exactness.** The suite has no hardcoded bit oracle for the LU *update*
path (`golden_bits.rs` covers the dense symmetric factor), so I built a
before/after harness digesting raw IEEE-754 bits of `ftran`/`btran` after
every update on the casctanks trace: identical (1008 steps,
`496c17cb930c9e11`). That trace never rolls back, so a strict-budget second
pass forced 19 `NeedsRefactor` rollbacks: also identical (1584 steps,
`12eb7d4263be3473`).

### Item E — post-amalgamation `Supernode.nrow` (`2484c27`)

Quantified before fixing, against `static_rows(i).len()`: **zero** error
without amalgamation, systematic undercount with it (never an overcount),
worst case 1844 vs 3083 summed — 40% low.

Fixed with a closed form rather than the issue's suggested seen-bitmap
(`find_supernodes` has only `col_counts`, not the pattern):
`merged_nrow = child_group_ncol + parent_group_nrow`, which Liu's
elimination-tree containment makes the exact union cardinality. Re-measured:
**zero error on every supernode**, 7 families x 3 `nemin`.

**Results identical, dispatch corrected** — exactly what the issue asked to be
checked. All 18 factor bit-digests and all 18 inertias unchanged across
9 matrices x 2 `nemin`. Consumers move: `estimate_assembly_flops` up 2-3x,
`peak_contrib_bytes` on grid40x40/`nemin`=32 from 3200 to 81808 bytes (25x
under-estimate of contribution-block memory). **One dispatch flip recorded**:
grid60x60/`nemin`=32 crosses `PAR_MIN_FLOPS` (4.3M -> 12.2M) and now routes
parallel — the precise failure mode the issue predicted.

Both `merge_flop_budget` guard sites corrected in lockstep.

## Decisions Made

Two entries appended to `dev/decisions.md`:

1. `Supernode.nrow` uses an exact closed-form union cardinality rather than a
   seen-bitmap or an upper bound; the `PAR_MIN_FLOPS` dispatch flip is an
   accepted consequence, and the `merge_flop_budget` sweep in
   `amalgamation-cost-model-2026-08-09.md` was taken under the old model so
   its numbers do not transfer.
2. Postorder child partitioning must be *stable* — an unstable two-pointer
   partition would feed `sort_unstable_by_key` a different input permutation
   and could silently change the fill-reducing ordering.
