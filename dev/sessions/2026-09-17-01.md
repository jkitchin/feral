## Goal

Explore issue #203 — pounce reports factorization cost growing `~n^2.3` and
symbolic fill `~n^1.37` on a long-horizon collocation optimal-control KKT
whose `dim K` and `nnz(K)` are exactly linear in the horizon. The issue
hypothesises a block chain with an available linear-fill elimination order
that no ordering backend is finding, and asks three questions plus a fourth
from the original report (why MA57 is 1.2–1.9x faster per iteration).

## Accomplished

### 1. Diagnosed the issue, and refuted its premise

The reproducer's own index arithmetic says the matrix is **not** a chain of
small blocks: the embedded spatial coupling is 775x775 with 1977 nonzeros
(2.55/row, a near-tree gas network) crossed with a time path of `3*nfe`
points. Spatial extent 775 exceeds temporal extent (18 to 288 over the
sweep), so a time-slice separator is the *expensive* cut.

Built the chain permutation the issue asks for and replayed it through
feral's symbolic pipeline as `External`. It is exactly linear (2.01x per
doubling — the issue's arithmetic is right) and **90x worse than `Auto` in
`nnz_L`, 3254x worse in flops at nfe=6**, 38x/135x at nfe=48. So ask #2 is
answered with the opposite sign, and ask #3 (a chain detector in `Auto`)
would be actively harmful. In `tried-and-rejected.md`.

### 2. Found the real gap, with an external oracle

Built an analysis-only MA57 probe (MA57ID + MA57AD), dumped its pivot order,
and replayed it through feral's own pipeline. Ordering alone:

| nfe | feral `Auto` flops | real-METIS perm through feral | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 | 1.83e9 | 1.72e9 | 1.06 |
| 48 | 1.09e10 | 6.42e9 | 1.70 |
| 96 | 5.06e10 | 1.75e10 | 2.89 |

Real METIS's flop exponent falls toward ~1.4 as the horizon grows; feral's
sat flat at 2.2–2.6. That gap alone covers the MA57 wall-clock advantage in
ask #4 without invoking any kernel difference.

Three candidate causes eliminated by measurement: the top-level separator
(577 vs 481 — 1.20x against a 3.26x gap), tuning (a five-knob sweep moves
nothing by more than 10%), and graph compression (224,646 distinct closed
neighbourhoods out of 224,646 vertices, so METIS's `CompressGraph` is a
no-op here). Confirmed the deficit is **general**, not pattern-specific:
`feral-metis` is ~1.4x behind real METIS on plain 2-D and 3-D grid
Laplacians too.

### 3. Fixed it

`feral-metis` refined the **edge cut** through uncoarsening and converted to
a vertex separator once at the finest level, then ran one positive-gain-only
greedy pass. METIS refines the **node separator itself** at every level. Two
compounding gaps: the wrong objective during uncoarsening, and a
hill-climbing-free greedy pass that stops at the first local minimum (which
is why `fm_passes=200` gave bit-identical output to `fm_passes=10`).

Added `refine_separator_fm` — one-sided FM with negative-gain moves, an undo
log, rollback to the lightest separator seen, and a hard balance bound — and
restructured `multilevel_node_bisection` to build the separator at the
coarsest level and refine it at every level. Gated on
`MetisOptions::node_refine`, now defaulting to `true`.

| graph | metis before | metis after | real METIS | gain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| collocation KKT n=112,326 | 7.71e9 | 3.05e9 | 1.72e9 | 2.52x |
| n=224,646 | 2.10e10 | **6.74e9** | 6.42e9 | 3.11x |
| n=449,286 | 6.89e10 | 2.91e10 | 1.75e10 | 2.37x |
| grid2d 300x300 | 4.69e8 | 4.27e8 | 3.33e8 | 1.10x |
| grid3d 40^3 | 2.31e10 | 2.18e10 | 1.67e10 | 1.06x |

At n=224,646 feral is within 5% of real METIS and its top separator matches
exactly (481 = 481).

**Evidence.** `cargo test --workspace`: 1199 passed, 0 failed, 25 ignored.
`cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`
clean. Six hand-oracled tests for the FM kernel (path -> 1 vertex, `k x k`
grid -> `k`, barbell -> 1, balance at four tolerances, monotonicity,
determinism) written before the implementation; two more for the driver.

## Unfavourable comparisons

Stated here rather than buried:

- **The plan's acceptance table was met on 1 of its 4 rows.** n=449,286
  (2.91e10 vs `<= 2.5e10`), grid2d (4.27e8 vs `<= 3.7e8`) and grid3d
  (2.18e10 vs `<= 1.9e10`) all missed; only n=224,646 beat its target. The
  default was flipped anyway — see `decisions.md`.
- **The grids barely moved.** 6–10% against 2.4–3.5x on the KKTs, and they
  remain ~1.3x behind real METIS. So on grid Laplacians something other than
  the refinement structure is giving away ~30%, and this session did not
  find it.
- **`Auto` is bit-identical to before, so pounce sees nothing by default.**
  `choose_adaptive` still reroutes every would-be-`MetisND` decision to
  `Amf` (#67/#73), leaving 1.74x on the table at n=449,286.
- **No wall-clock was measured.** Everything above is symbolic. The predicted
  speedup is a forecast.
- **`bench` was vacuous** — see the note at the top.

## Decisions Made

- `MetisOptions::node_refine` defaults to `true` despite the acceptance miss
  (appended to `dev/decisions.md`).
- `choose_adaptive` is deliberately **not** touched. The #67/#73 override
  rests on real factor+solve wall-clock across the IPM corpus, and
  `tried-and-rejected.md` already records a fill-guarded race rejected
  because fill does not predict speed (nql180: 0.98x the fill under MetisND,
  still 2.05x slower end to end). Re-deciding it on symbolic fill would
  repeat that mistake.

## Abandoned Approaches

- The chain / front-to-back time ordering for collocation KKTs, and the
  chain-detection heuristic proposed for `Auto` (appended to
  `dev/tried-and-rejected.md` with the numbers).
- `issue203_sep_tree`, a probe that reverse-engineered the whole separator
  tree from a permutation. Correct at depth 0, wrong below it; removed
  rather than shipped (commit `7045060`).
