## Reading
The test was written under #215. It checks that the #215 per-front
budget (`delayed_capacity`) does not force CB on this matrix. #221 later
added a second, independent trigger on purpose (bounding carry cost on
qcqp1000-2c / nql180). The assertion `n_cascade_break_nodes == 0` counts
both triggers, so #221's intended behavior trips a #215 check, even
though the answer is exact and no pivot escaped the threshold bound.

## Options considered
1. Split the counter by trigger. The test keeps zero budget-triggered CB,
   exact inertia and zero floored pivots. (Chosen by the user.)
2. Drop the CB-count assertion. Rejected: a pure relaxation that no
   longer says which trigger fired.
3. Retune DELAY_CHAIN_GROWTH (the 10.4 ratio sits just above 10).
   Rejected: moves #221's tuning without a wrong answer to justify it.
