* 13:30 :mc64:symbolic-skip:design:
Took on the dense-column follow-up "safe win" (audit §8.1 option (b), user
request): skip the speculative MC64 in symbolic LdltCompress when the resolved
scaling won't reuse the cache. Established two design facts by reading
solver.rs + symbolic/mod.rs + ldlt_compress.rs:
- Symbolic runs at factor() Step 3 (solver.rs:805), on a cache MISS only;
  it is cached per pattern fingerprint, so LdltCompress MC64 runs ONCE per
  pattern. Scaling resolves later (Step 3.75/3.8, lines 942/962). To make
  symbolic scaling-aware we'd compute pick_scaling_strategy before the
  symbolic call and plumb a will_reuse_mc64 bool in. Feasible.
- BUT the MC64 in LdltCompress is LOAD-BEARING for compression, not
  speculative: build_supermap (ldlt_compress.rs:39-77) walks the MC64 matching
  permutation's cycle structure to form super-variables (MUMPS ICNTL(12)=2 /
  Duff-Pralet). Skipping MC64 = skipping compression = changing the ordering.
  So the win is only real if compression isn't worth its MC64 cost — an
  empirical question. Decided to measure before implementing.

* 14:00 :cost-benefit:negative-result:
Wrote probe_compress_costbenefit_argv (symbolic+numeric, None vs LdltCompress,
5-run median, release). Measured the large dense-column + InfNorm bucket. The
premise is REFUTED. Crux = ROSEPETAL vs ORTHREGF, both large near-dense-column
InfNorm, opposite verdicts:
- ROSEPETAL n=3000 deg=2001: tot_None=5.97s, tot_Compress=1.45s, -75.7%.
  Compression pays 0.68s MC64 but the compressed ordering gives an 8x NUMERIC
  speedup (5.72s -> 0.77s). Reproducible (-75.7%, -75.0%).
- ORTHREGF n=6405 deg=1601: tot_None=6.2ms, tot_Compress=11.9ms, +91.8%.
  MC64 pure overhead, zero numeric benefit. Reproducible (+91.8%, +89.4%).
INDEFM/ex8_2_3 neutral (~+2%), ex8_2_2 -0.5%, SINQUAD2 +5.7%, small matrices
sub-ms.
Conclusion: the value of LdltCompress is its NUMERIC FILL REDUCTION, which is
independent of the scaling choice and unpredicted by max_col_deg / MC64 cost /
n (ROSEPETAL's MC64 is 68x ORTHREGF's, yet ROSEPETAL is the win). The
scaling-reuse signal carries NO information about whether compression pays off.
A gate keyed on it would regress the fill wins (ROSEPETAL ~4x, ex8_2_2) to save
ms on the overhead-only losses. NOT a safe win. Rejected; not implemented.
