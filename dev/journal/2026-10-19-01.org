* 2026-10-21 13:00 :issue-221:parity:bk:review:
Review finding, confirmed: 676df22 broke blocked/scalar bit-parity on
fronts that cannot delay (may_delay=false, u > 0). The blocked kernel ran
two phases (delay pass, then force_inline pass over the stuck columns)
while factor_frontal stayed single-pass and force-accepted in place. On
main the two are byte-identical; at 676df22..ed292fc forced_front(m)
(m = 8/40/300, ForceAccept and PerturbToEps) differed in perm, L, D and
contrib (reviewer: contrib up to 4.5e-8). The blocked phase 2 also made
panel-position-dependent choices (forced 1x1 whenever the inline 2x2 was
not allowed, e.g. swap needed at c > 0), so no single scalar policy
matched it.
Fix: one forced-pass policy F = scalar_pivot_step(may_delay=false,
allow_rook=false), used by both kernels.
- Scalar driver: same two phases (delay pass with delay_swap_to_boundary,
  then F over [nelim, ncol) in order).
- Blocked phase 2: every scalar step (tail, fallback) is F. The
  force_inline panel commits a 1x1 at col only when F provably does: BK
  argmax r not fully summed and either (a) no fully-summed entry in the
  column, or (b) partner col+1 (a[col,col+1] != 0), peeked, fails the
  same growth/det gates (then LAPACK test and partner path both end in
  the 1x1 at col). (b) leaves col+1 pre-peeked; the next peek replays only
  pivot c-1 (new peek_ahead_replay_from). Bails (r_fs partner, passing
  2x2, refine-flag ambiguity, static floor, Fail mode) go to F.

  4 sizes x 2 eps x 3 seeds x 3 floors). On ed292fc: 4/4 original tests
