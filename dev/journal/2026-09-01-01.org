* 11:55 :krylov:analysis:preconditioner:
Separate thread: evaluating ~/Desktop/feral-krylov.org, a proposal to
add recycled MINRES to feral. Checked its central numerical claim
before reading any code -- the proposal offers the absolute-value
factor preconditioner M = P Ds^-1 L |D| L^T Ds^-1 P^T as the SPD
preconditioner MINRES needs.

Built a 55x55 saddle KKT with a barrier diagonal spanning 1e-6..1e6,
factored with scipy's Bunch-Kaufman LDL, formed |D| by eigendecomposing
each 1x1/2x2 pivot block, and examined M^-1 K:

  eigenvalues of M^-1 K: min = -1.000000  max = 1.000000
  distinct |eigenvalues|: [1.]   (35 at +1, 20 at -1)
  ||(M^-1 K)^2 - I||_F = 1.5e-10

So M^-1 K = sign-similar to identity: its minimal polynomial has degree
2 and preconditioned MINRES converges in at most TWO iterations in
exact arithmetic. Verified against scipy MINRES: 2 iterations at every
barrier value tested when the factorization is of the current matrix.

Implication for the proposal. With an exact, current-matrix
factorization the preconditioner is so strong that no Krylov subspace
of any size is built -- which means there is nothing to extract Ritz
vectors FROM. Phase 1 (the |D| preconditioner) and Phase 2 (recycling)
undercut each other; the proposal does not note this.

* 12:05 :krylov:analysis:stale:
Follow-up: the only regime where the Krylov layer does real work is an
INEXACT preconditioner, i.e. a factorization frozen at an earlier IPM
iterate. That is also the only regime with a real prize, since it saves
factorization time rather than solve time. Tested it -- 80x80 KKT,
factorization frozen at mu=1e-1, applied to later mu with 12 variables
driven toward their bounds:

  mu     cond(K)   MINRES its   spectrum of M0^-1 K
  1e-1   1.99e+05      2        [1.00e+00, 1.00e+00]
  1e-2   2.66e+04    200+       [1.97e-02, 1.14e+03]
  1e-3   2.15e+05    200+       [1.47e-02, 9.50e+03]
  1e-6   5.46e+07    200+       [1.41e-02, 2.50e+06]

Control refactorizing at every mu: 2 iterations throughout.

One frozen factorization is already useless one barrier decade later --
the spectrum blows from {±1} to five orders of magnitude. CAVEAT: this
is a synthetic model with an aggressive 10x mu drop per step and a
harsh active-set model; real consecutive IPM matrices are closer
together than this. It bounds the sensitivity rather than settling the
question, and the honest read is that factorization reuse needs
measurement on captured pounce sequences, not that it is dead. Recorded
here so the eventual Phase 0 does not re-derive it.
