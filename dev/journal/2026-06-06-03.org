* 19:10 :impossibility:proof:
Analyzed the TIGHTEST possible column bound. For the matched column j2 of a
popped row q0: complementary slackness gives v[j2]=cost[jperm[j2]]-u[q0], and
dual feasibility makes that edge the column's reduced-cost minimum. So
lb_tight[j2]=cost[jperm[j2]]-u[q0]. With vj=dq0-cost[jperm[j2]]+u[q0]:
  vj + lb_tight = dq0.
Skip fires iff vj+lb_tight≥csp ⟺ dq0≥csp, but q0 was popped only because
dq0<csp. ⇒ a column-level reduced-cost bound can NEVER prune, at any
tightness. The matched edge always sits exactly at dq0; the scan exists to
find improving edges to *other* rows. Maintaining v live would not help.
