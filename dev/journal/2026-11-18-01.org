* 18:20 :issue-237:permute:signed-zero:finding:
Writing the bitwise test (warm vs cold `permute_csc_values` oracle)
exposed a pre-existing difference: the cold path (`from_triplets`) keeps
a -0.0 input as -0.0 (bits 0x8000000000000000), the 0.23.0 warm path
(`vec![0.0]` then `+=`) yields +0.0. Not introduced here. Kept the warm
behaviour (in-place write is `0.0 + v`) so warm refactorizations stay
bit-identical to 0.23.0; the test normalizes signed zeros in the oracle
and pins +0.0 on the warm path. A signed zero cannot change inertia
(zero pivots are classified by magnitude), so not chased further.
