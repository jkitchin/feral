* 13:40 :release:docs:
Found a real docs defect while preparing the release, not a cosmetic
one. The SparseLuSymbolic module header and all four constructors
(analyze, analyze_with, analyze_triangularized, analyze_amd_only) argue
at length about which ordering to prefer and what the default costs.
Every one of those comments was written when the ordering computed there
was the one SparseLu::factor used. Under the #171 default it is not read
at all beyond a dimension check. Shipping 0.16.0 with those comments
unchanged would document a decision the library no longer makes.

Added the note to all five sites plus LuParams::dense_bump_max_dim (now
gated on GilbertPeierls AND analyze_triangularized AND a non-zero cap --
inert twice over under the shipped defaults).

warnings against a stashed baseline: same three warnings in the touched
files, at shifted line numbers (159->189, 190->225, 9->18).
