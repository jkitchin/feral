fhnw-binschedule1 KKT (n=1 914 525), prefix-only probe, preprocess None:

| AMD      | 4.369e8  |  24 445      | 5.56e12  | 86.3 s     |

Prefix HWM 1.3–1.9 GB. AMF's ordering is 32× AMD's fill, so the issue's 11 GB
abort is symbolic_finish building structure for a 1.4e10-entry factor: a bad
ordering, not a leak. Degree profile: median 5, p99 54, 8 692 cols > 100,
216 > 1000, 4 cols at 56 842 and 2 at 454 823 (only those 6 exceed the
dense threshold 13 836).

Primal2 full production path (symbolic_factorize_with_method, default
params): Auto → Amd/LdltCompress nnz(L)=3.692e8 peak 8.30 GB (issue: 3.72e8,
8.33 GB — reproduced). Amf/LdltCompress nnz(L)=2.079e8 peak 3.53 GB.

Root cause: AMF RMF quantization (algo.rs re-insertion): rmf ≥ i32::MAX-1 is
stored as round(rmf/n). At n=1.9M a score of 3e9 becomes ~1 600, i.e. a
near-dense variable is filed as *cheap* — a priority inversion. mumps-expert
confirmed this is verbatim MUMPS 5.8.2 HAMF4 (ana_orderings.F:4985-4994, also
MUMPS_CST_AMF :8074-8079; WF is 32-bit INTEGER, no guard, no 64-bit variant).
So feral is a faithful port of a MUMPS quirk that only bites at large n with
high-degree rows (RMF can exceed 2^31 once degrees reach ~27k).

Experiment (local, reverted): monotone quantization — store round(rmf) as
i64 (wf is already Vec<i64>), no /n fall-through.
  fhnw    AMF: nnz(L) 1.388e10 → 1.835e8, max colcount 10 623, Σc² 5.11e11,
               order time 127.6 s. Now 2.4× less fill and 11× fewer flops
               than AMD, 2.2× / 7× better than MetisND.
  Primal2 AMF: unchanged (1.802e8) — the overflow branch never fires there.
Conclusion: one root cause behind the fhnw half of #246. With monotone
quantization, Auto → AMF becomes the best choice on fhnw, and AMF is also
the cheap fix for Primal2 (vs AMD) — the latter needs the powerflow22
regression check (not available in this container; GAMS-licensed, lives in
pounce). The fix deviates from MUMPS, so the AMF corpus oracle
(tests/amf_corpus_oracle.rs, MUMPS HAMF4 sidecars, 10% nnz_L margin) may move
on large high-degree matrices; needs a decisions.md entry.
