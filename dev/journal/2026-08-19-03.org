* 19:55 :issue-175:mechanism:
Where the per-call cost actually lives, read off =cb_run_parallel=
(src/numeric/solve.rs): the forward pass takes the *shared*
=contribs= mutex twice per front (once per child drained, once to store
its own block) — =ctx.contribs.lock()= inside the per-front loop — plus a
=scratch= mutex per task and two =rayon::scope=s per solve. The
=contribs= traffic is O(n_nodes) and contended by every worker, which is
what 45,736 supernodes × 14 threads turns into 3M involuntary context
switches. The work available to hide it is =total= (Σ nrow·(nelim+1)).

So the overhead the gate is missing is *per front*, not per task: it
scales with the number of supernodes, while every term in the current
gate is a property of the tree's shape.

* 20:35 :issue-175:finding:
The reporter's suggested signature — =total / fwd_seeds.len()= below a
floor — does not separate the data. =poisson_160= at 8 workers has
1.09M/50 = 21.8k per seed and *wins* 0.73; =NARX_CFy= has ~11 seeds over
~1.4M ≈ 127k per seed and loses. A per-seed floor that rejected NARX_CFy
would reject the winners first. Work per *front* is the discriminator,
which matches the mechanism (a mutex acquisition per front, not per
task). Recorded in dev/tried-and-rejected.md.
