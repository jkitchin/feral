* 09:18 :lu:sparse:update:prior-art:correctness:
PRIOR-ART GATE FIRED. The "asymptotic fix = textbook FT cyclic permutation
(Hessenberg, eliminate single subdiagonal)" was ALREADY TRIED AND REVERTED — journal
2026-06-08-01.org (19:00 + 21:00). Evidence: in a sparse U the Hessenberg's diagonal
pivots are the OLD superdiagonal entries U[k,k+1], frequently zero → zero pivot →
division by zero. The current in-place + partial-pivoting eliminate_bump exists
PRECISELY to dodge that landmine (sub-diagonal spike entry is a nonzero pivot; swap
goes into the eta so unit-lower L is never permuted).

Also already documented (2026-06-08, lines 232-234): the O(bump²) DENSE-spike case is
the "inherent worst case; the FT win is for localized spikes (the realistic LP
regime)." casctanks McCormick relaxations are the non-localized counterexample.

Implication: cannot naively implement the cyclic permutation. The two correct
asymptotic routes (journal) are symmetric-permutation FT (stored-state bookkeeping —
permuting U staleness the L σᵀ relation) or sparse BGR (Reid 1982) — both "substantial,
delicate." That is a research spike, not a recipe.
